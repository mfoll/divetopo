# DiveTopo v1.5.0

This release adds four fully published Mediterranean dive sites and consolidates the regional maps introduced in v1.4.

## New dive sites

- **Impérial du Milieu – Riou**, Bouches-du-Rhône
- **Pointe Escampobariou**, Var Centre
- **Sec des Suisses**, Var Est
- **Cap Gros**, Alpes-Maritimes

Each site includes two 2D maps, calibrated 3D views, printable boards and an interactive terrain package.

## Cartography improvements

- widened validated Mediterranean extents and recalibrated initial 3D viewpoints;
- corrected the coastal bathymetric transition at Grande Baie;
- consolidated Plate aux Mérous and Pierre du Jas into the wider Les Magnons coverage;
- consolidated Sec du Langoustier into Sec de la Jeaune Garde;
- refined regional labels, connectors, site markers and calculated scale bars;
- corrected the French wording for Alpes-Maritimes pages;
- added the GitHub link to the homepage header.

## Quality and tooling

- local reusable 3D camera-calibration workflow, excluded from production builds;
- full-resolution native-map and board inspection;
- desktop and mobile route checks in French and English;
- 3D terrain, attribution, overflow and regional-label checks;
- automated release contract ensuring all sites announced for the release are actually published.
